# Building Fire & Wildfire Evacuation Guidelines

## Overview
Fires spread rapidly within minutes, producing intense heat and lethal toxic smoke. Early detection, immediate evacuation, and smoke inhalation prevention are crucial.

## Residential & Commercial Fire Safety
1. Install smoke detectors on every floor and test them monthly. Replace batteries annually.
2. Maintain accessible, working fire extinguishers (ABC rating) near kitchen and exit areas. Know the PASS method: Pull the pin, Aim low, Squeeze lever, Sweep side to side.
3. Establish a primary and secondary escape route from every room in your home.

## During a Structural Fire (Immediate Evacuation)
1. GET OUT, STAY OUT. Never delay evacuation to gather personal possessions.
2. Crawl low under smoke. Heavy toxic smoke rises toward the ceiling; air near the floor is cooler and cleaner.
3. Test door handles and doors with the back of your hand before opening. If hot to touch, do NOT open; use an alternate escape route.
4. If clothes catch fire: STOP, DROP, and ROLL. Cover your face with hands, drop to the ground, and roll back and forth until flames are extinguished.
5. If trapped in a room: Close all doors between you and the fire. Seal door cracks and vents with wet towels or clothes. Call emergency services and wave a brightly colored cloth or flashlight out the window.

## Wildfire Response Protocols
1. Prepare your property: Clear dried vegetation, dead leaves, and combustible materials within a 30-foot defensible zone around your home.
2. If a wildfire threatens your area, pack your emergency vehicle immediately with your Go-Bag, masks, and essential documents.
3. Close all doors, windows, and fireplace dampers to prevent ember entry. Turn off propane tanks.
4. Wear long-sleeved wool or cotton clothing, heavy leather boots, and N95 masks or wet bandanas to filter ash particles.
5. Follow local official evacuation orders immediately. Do not wait until flames are visible.
