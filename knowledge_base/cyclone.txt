# Tropical Cyclone, Hurricane & Severe Storm Action Guide

## Overview
Cyclones and hurricanes bring high-velocity winds, torrential rainfall, storm surges, and widespread infrastructure damage. Advanced preparation saves lives.

## Pre-Cyclone Readiness (Warning Phase)
1. Track meteorological weather alerts and coastal cyclone warnings issued by official weather departments.
2. Secure roof sheets, board up windows, or reinforce glass surfaces with storm shutters or plywood.
3. Trim weak branches and dead trees near house structures.
4. Charge all mobile devices, power banks, battery radios, and emergency lighting. Store extra fresh drinking water in sanitized containers.
5. Secure loose outdoor objects such as outdoor furniture, garbage bins, and tools that could become dangerous flying projectiles.

## During a Cyclone (Impact Phase)
1. Remain indoors in the safest, central room of your home, away from windows, glass doors, and exterior walls.
2. Turn off gas supplies and main electrical circuit breakers if power outages occur or flooding starts.
3. Do NOT go outside during the "eye" of the cyclone. When wind suddenly calms down, it is temporary; destructive winds will resume shortly from the opposite direction.
4. Stay tuned to battery-powered radios for official updates and emergency bulletins.

## Post-Cyclone Safety
1. Beware of fallen power lines, electrocution risks, damaged gas pipelines, and weakened trees or buildings.
2. Report downed lines and gas leaks to authorities immediately.
3. Boil drinking water before consumption to prevent waterborne disease outbreaks.
4. Inspect roof structures, electrical connections, and foundation walls before re-entering evacuated properties.
