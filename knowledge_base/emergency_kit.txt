# Emergency Survival Kit & Essential Go-Bag Preparation Guide

## Overview
An emergency kit (Go-Bag) is a portable container containing essential survival items designed to sustain a family or individual for a minimum of 72 hours during a disaster evacuation.

## Core Emergency Supplies Checklist
1. Water: 1 gallon (3.78 liters) per person per day for drinking and sanitation (3-day minimum supply for evacuation, 14-day supply for home shelter).
2. Food: 3-day supply of non-perishable, high-energy food items (canned protein, dried fruit, energy bars, MREs) with a manual can opener.
3. First-Aid Kit: Sterile gauze pads, bandage strips, adhesive tape, antiseptic wipes, burn ointment, scissors, tweezers, CPR face shield, and disposable gloves.
4. Essential Medications: Minimum 7-day supply of prescription medications, inhalers, insulin, and basic over-the-counter pain relievers/antihistamines.
5. Light & Power: High-lumen LED flashlight, headlamps, extra lithium batteries, solar/crank emergency radio (NOAA compatible), and high-capacity USB power banks (20,000mAh+).
6. Communication Tools: Multi-band emergency radio, whistle to signal for help, permanent marker, waterproof notebook, and printed list of emergency contacts.
7. Sanitation & Personal Hygiene: Wet wipes, hand sanitizer, heavy-duty trash bags, toilet paper, feminine hygiene products, N95 dust masks, and liquid soap.
8. Tools & Hardware: Multi-tool with pliers and knife, duct tape, 50ft paracord, leather work gloves, emergency matches in a waterproof container, and protective safety goggles.
9. Documents & Currency: Waterproof container holding copies of photo IDs, passports, birth certificates, medical records, insurance policies, bank cards, and emergency cash (small bills).
10. Shelter & Warmth: Emergency thermal Mylar blankets, compact rain ponchos, change of heavy clothing, sturdy waterproof boots, and sleeping bags.

## Maintenance Protocol
- Inspect Go-Bag contents every 6 months.
- Replace expired food, water, batteries, and prescription drugs twice a year during seasonal daylight saving changes.
