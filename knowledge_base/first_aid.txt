# Crisis Emergency First-Aid Guidelines & Resuscitation

## Overview
Immediate first-aid care during disasters stabilizes injured individuals until professional paramedic and emergency medical services arrive.

## Basic Life Support & CPR
1. Assess Safety: Ensure the scene is safe for you and the victim before approaching.
2. Check Responsiveness: Tap shoulders firmly and ask loudly, "Are you okay?".
3. Call Emergency Services: Call local emergency services (e.g. 112 / 911 / 108) or ask a bystander to call and fetch an Automated External Defibrillator (AED).
4. Perform Chest Compressions (Hands-Only CPR):
   - Place heel of one hand in center of victim's chest, place other hand on top.
   - Push hard and fast at a rate of 100 to 120 compressions per minute to the beat of "Stayin' Alive".

## Managing Severe Bleeding
1. Apply Direct Pressure: Firmly press sterile gauze or clean cloth directly over the wound.
2. Maintain Continuous Pressure: Do not lift the cloth to check the wound; add more absorbent layers if blood soaks through.
3. Apply Pressure Bandage: Secure gauze with a tight bandage wrapper. If severe limb bleeding persists, apply a commercial tourniquet 2 to 3 inches above the wound.

## Treating Burns (Thermal & Chemical)
1. Cool Burns: Hold minor burn area under cool running water for 10 to 15 minutes. Do NOT apply ice, butter, or oil.
2. Cover Protectively: Cover loosely with sterile non-stick bandage or clean plastic wrap.
3. Seek Immediate Medical Help for major burns covering large surface areas or face/hands.

## Managing Fractures & Sprains
1. Immobilize: Keep the injured limb still. Do NOT attempt to realign broken bones.
2. Apply Splint: Secure rigid board or folded magazine alongside the limb using cloth strips.
3. Cold Compress: Apply ice pack wrapped in a cloth to reduce swelling.
