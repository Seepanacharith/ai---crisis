# Disaster Evacuation Protocol & Shelter Navigation

## Overview
Evacuation orders are issued by local emergency authorities to safeguard lives against impending hazards such as severe flooding, wildfire encroachment, hazardous chemical spills, or approaching cyclones.

## Step-by-Step Evacuation Plan
1. Know Official Evacuation Zones: Learn your district's designated evacuation zones and pre-determined emergency assembly routes.
2. Maintain Vehicle Readiness: Keep vehicle gas tanks at least half-full at all times during storm warning seasons. Keep emergency tools and Go-Bags in the trunk.
3. Secure the Property Before Departing:
   - Turn off main utilities (electricity, water, gas) if instructed.
   - Lock all exterior windows and doors.
   - Unplug sensitive electronics.
4. Follow Official Routes: Stick strictly to designated evacuation highways. Do NOT take shortcuts through unverified backroads, which may be blocked or inundated by floodwaters.
5. Family Communication Plan: Designate an out-of-state or out-of-city contact person whom all family members can notify via SMS once safe.

## Shelter Etiquette & Safety
- Upon arrival at a designated emergency shelter or relief camp, register immediately with administration.
- Respect medical triage, food distribution guidelines, and sanitation rules.
- Notify shelter medical personnel of any chronic health conditions, allergies, or special care requirements.
